2. Principle of data minimization

Protecting your data is important to us. The app processes only data that is technically necessary for operation and collects as little personal data as possible. The app does not analyze user behavior, does not contain advertising and does not use tracking.

3. Data processed by the app

3.1 When the app starts

To manage in-app purchases, the app initializes the service RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). In this context, an anonymous, randomly generated user identifier, a device identifier (for example IDFV) and the purchase status are processed and transmitted to RevenueCat. Names, e-mail addresses and payment data are not transmitted to RevenueCat by us. Payment processing itself is handled exclusively by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

3.2 Loading additional images

When additional images are loaded, only with Premium, the app first requests image information from our server api.coloringpictures.de. The actual image files are then loaded directly from our image hosting provider Modal Labs, Inc. (*.modal.run, USA). For technical reasons, the IP address of your device is transmitted to the respective server. No further personal data is sent.

3.3 Photo library

The app accesses the photo library only if you save a colored image. The images remain on your device.

3.4 Checking for app updates

The app uses the service Expo (EAS Update, u.expo.dev, USA) to check whether a new version is available. For technical reasons, the IP address and a randomly generated installation identifier are transmitted. No further personal data is sent.

3.5 AI-generated graphics, icons and coloring pages

Some graphics, icons and coloring pages were created using generative AI and were subsequently selected, reviewed and partially edited. They contain no personal data.

4. Legal bases

Processing is carried out on the basis of Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the user agreement, in particular in-app purchases) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in technically error-free provision of the app). Processing based on consent does not take place; the app processes only data that is technically necessary for operation. Purchases and other actions that are sensitive for children are additionally protected by an adult task (Parental Gate).

5. Recipients and disclosure

Personal data is not sold and is not disclosed for advertising purposes. Recipients of technically necessary data are:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. – purchase management,
  • Apple App Store or Google Play – payment processing,
  • Expo – EAS Update and app updates,
  • our server api.coloringpictures.de, hosted by IONOS SE in Germany,
  • Modal Labs, Inc. – image hosting.

6. International data transfers

RevenueCat, Inc., Expo, Modal Labs, Inc. as well as Apple and Google also process data in the USA. The transfer to Modal Labs, Inc. and RevenueCat, Inc. is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. The transfer to Expo, Apple and Google is based on their certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. Storage period

Data stored locally on the device, such as saved and colored images and settings, remains stored until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Data processed by RevenueCat and the app stores is subject to their respective retention periods.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, an e-mail to coloringpicturesforkids@gmail.com is sufficient.

9. Permissions

To save your colored images, the app uses the sharing/saving function of your device. Through this function, you can save the image to the photo library. The app does not access location, camera, contacts or microphone.

10. Children and parents

This app is expressly intended for children and families. It is designed so that we do not collect direct identity data from children, such as names, e-mail addresses, postal addresses, precise location, contacts, photos or payment data.

For technically necessary functions, the data described in Section 3 may be processed, in particular an anonymous user identifier and technical purchase data for purchase management through RevenueCat, as well as the IP address when loading additional images from our server and our image hosting provider. This data is not used for advertising purposes and is not used to personally identify children.

In-app purchases, opening external links and sharing images are protected by an adult task (Parental Gate).

Content stored locally in the app, such as saved and colored images and settings, remains on the device and can be deleted at any time in the app or by uninstalling the app.

If images are shared using the sharing function, this only happens after the Parental Gate and through the app or function selected on the device.

Parents may contact us at any time at coloringpagesforkids@gmail.com if they have questions about data protection or wish to exercise their rights.

Additional information for users in the USA: This app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Persistent identifiers, such as IDFV, are used exclusively to support internal operations, including purchase management and technical functionality, and not for behavioral advertising. No data from children is requested beyond what is necessary for the functionality of the app. Parents may object to further collection or use and request access and deletion by sending an e-mail to coloringpagesforkids@gmail.com. In practice, we do not store identifying data about children ourselves. Content stored locally on the device can be deleted directly in the app or by uninstalling the app. Pseudonymous technical purchase data at RevenueCat can be deleted upon request, where necessary by using the purchase receipt for assignment. Since the app does not collect identifying data, we generally cannot identify individual children in the data (see Article 11 GDPR); therefore, no further access or deletion by us is usually possible.
