2. Principle of data minimization

Protecting your data is important to us. The app processes only the data that is technically necessary to operate the app. The app does not contain advertising, does not use advertising tracking, and does not analyze user behavior for marketing purposes.

3. Data processed by the app

3.1 When starting and using the app

To technically manage in-app purchases and subscriptions, the app uses the service RevenueCat, Inc.. In this context, the following technical data may be processed:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated user identifier,
  • a technical device identifier, for example IDFV on iOS,
  • purchase and subscription status,
  • product identifiers of purchased or subscribed content.

We do not transmit your name, e-mail address, postal address, credit card details or bank details to RevenueCat. Payment processing is carried out exclusively by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

3.2 In-app purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed via the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment processing is carried out exclusively by Apple or Google. We do not receive complete payment data, credit card data or bank details.

The app uses RevenueCat to check and manage purchase and subscription status. RevenueCat processes technical purchase information so that premium features can be unlocked and the subscription status can be verified.

Refund requests are handled exclusively by the respective app store provider. We do not influence refund decisions and do not transmit usage or consumption data to Apple or Google for this purpose.

3.3 Loading additional images

When additional images, especially premium images, are loaded, the app retrieves images from our server at api.coloringpictures.de. For technical reasons, the IP address of your device is transmitted to the server so that the requested images can be delivered to your device.

No further personal data such as name, e-mail address or precise location is sent to our server when images are loaded.

3.4 AI-generated coloring pictures

The app provides coloring pictures that were or may be generated using an AI model. No personal data entered by you is processed for this purpose. The images are generated generally or randomly and may vary in content.

3.5 Photo library and saving images

The app accesses the photo library only if you choose to save a colored image on your device. Access is used solely for this purpose. The saved images remain on your device.

The app does not access your location, camera, contacts or microphone.

3.6 Locally stored data

The app may store data locally on your device, for example:

  • saved or favorite images,
  • colored images,
  • app settings,
  • information about which content is locally available.

This data remains on your device and is not analyzed for advertising purposes. You can delete local data by removing it in the app or by uninstalling the app.

4. Purposes of processing

Data is processed for the following purposes:

  • providing the app functions,
  • unlocking and managing premium content,
  • checking purchase and subscription status,
  • technically delivering additional images,
  • saving colored images on the user's device upon request,
  • ensuring the technical functionality and security of the app.

5. Legal bases

Where personal data is processed, the processing is based on the following legal bases:

  • Article 6(1)(b) GDPR – performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures, especially for in-app purchases, subscriptions and premium functions.
  • Article 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in the technical provision, security and functionality of the app.
  • Article 6(1)(a) GDPR – consent, where consent is required, for example for certain device permissions.
  • Article 8 GDPR – where data of children is processed and consent is required, processing takes place only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

6. Recipients and disclosure of data

Personal data is not sold and is not shared for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data is disclosed only where technically necessary to provide the app functions. Recipients may include:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. – technical management of purchases and subscriptions,
  • Apple App Store – payment processing on iOS devices,
  • Google Play – payment processing on Android devices,
  • our image server api.coloringpictures.de – delivery of additional images.

7. International data transfers

RevenueCat, Apple and Google may also process data in the United States or in other countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area.

Where data is transferred to a third country, such transfer is based on appropriate data protection safeguards, in particular EU Standard Contractual Clauses, an adequacy decision of the European Commission, or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, where the respective provider participates in it.

8. Storage period

Data stored locally on your device remains stored until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

Data processed by RevenueCat, Apple or Google in connection with purchases and subscriptions is subject to the respective retention periods of these providers.

Technical data processed by our server, in particular IP addresses when images are retrieved, is processed only for as long as necessary for technical delivery, security and error analysis.

9. Children and parental consent

This app is intended for children and families. Minors should use the app only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

For children below the applicable minimum age for independent data protection consent, any processing that requires consent takes place only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. In the EU, this minimum age is between 13 and 16 years, depending on the member state.

Purchases and the opening of external links may be protected by an adult task (parental gate).

We do not knowingly collect names, e-mail addresses, precise location data, contacts, photos or other direct identity data from children.

Parents may contact us at any time by e-mail at coloringpagesforkids@gmail.com to request information about data processed in relation to their child or to request deletion.

10. Permissions

The app uses only the permissions necessary for the respective function.

  • Photo library: used only to save colored images on the device.
  • Internet connection: used to load additional images and to verify purchases.

The app does not access location, camera, contacts or microphone.
